The fine print & what I checked

  • Estimates, not quotes. All-in = flights + ~3 nights in Rome + onboard spend, for four. Per-person = total ÷ 4. 2027 fares move; final pricing confirmed at booking.
  • The age rule that shaped this. Carson turns 16 on Jun 23, 2027, so every sailing departs on/after ~Jun 24. Viking, Oceania, and Windstar are adults-only (18+) — why they're not here.
  • Cabins. Two adjacent staterooms throughout, no interiors; the kids' cabin is cross-referenced to yours to satisfy the under-21 rules.
  • Passports. All four valid past the trip — yours and Kristin's to 2032, Katelyn's and Carson's to May 2028. No renewals needed.
  • Airfare. Summer is peak DFW–Rome; American flies it nonstop, so the round-trip-Rome itineraries (Star Clippers, Regent) save travel time.
  • Early-booking savings. Star Clippers and Azamara have early-booking discounts that could lower these; Regent already reflects a 2-for-1 structure.

The honest take

Why you'll love it

  • By far the lowest price for the four of you.
  • Endless choice aboard — nobody gets bored.
  • Near-adult kids roam freely on a big ship.

Option 02 · The Adventure

Star Clippers

Star Clipper (tall ship) · 166 guests · 7 nights, round-trip Rome
All-in · family of 4
~$23,000
Per person
~$5,750

Not a cruise ship — an actual four-masted sailing clipper with just 166 guests. Help haul the sails, climb to the crow's nest, kayak off the back deck. It slips into tiny harbors and sails past the smoking cone of Stromboli. The one built for you and Carson — and it loops back to Rome, so you fly nonstop both ways.

The sailing
  • Sample dateMid-July 2027
  • Length7 nights + 3 in Rome
  • RouteRound-trip Rome (Civitavecchia)
  • Staterooms2 outside cabins, adjacent
  • Carson at 16✓ with an adult in-cabin
  • FlightNonstop DFW ⇄ Rome
What's included
Casual-elegant and unpackaged — drinks à la carte, or add the All-In package (~$99/person/day).
  • All meals — open seating, anytime
  • Water sports off the stern: kayaks, paddleboards, snorkeling
  • Tendering ashore where the ship anchors out
  • Sail-handling, knot-tying, mast-climb, navigation talks
  • Local musicians brought aboard in port

The honest take

Why you'll love it

  • A genuine sailing adventure — yours and Carson's dream, hands-on.

The "decide once and stop thinking about money" option. Regent's fare is the most all-inclusive at sea: every shore excursion, all dining, premium drinks, gratuities, Wi-Fi, transfers — and your flights.
