Trip Design · Iceland

The Ring Road

Ten days, one loop, a new landscape every morning — waterfalls and black-sand coast, a glacier lagoon, the volcanic north, and Snæfellsnes to close, all under 20+ hours of daylight.

The day-by-day

Built around a couple of two-night bases so it isn't a new hotel every night. Drive times are with-stops estimates.

1ReykjavíkAirport 45 min

Land & ease in

Pick up the car and shake off the flight with a soak — Sky Lagoon in the city or the Blue Lagoon near the airport. Evening in Reykjavík: Hallgrímskirkja, the old harbor, a good first dinner.

Stay: Skuggi Hotel · Sand Hotel · The Retreat at Blue Lagoon
2Golden Circle → South~3.5 hr

The classic trio, then the coast

Þingvellir rift valley, the Geysir hot-spring field, and thundering Gullfoss. Drop to the South Coast; optional Secret Lagoon or Kerið crater en route.

Stay: Hótel Hella · Hotel Rangá (gourmet + hot tubs)
3South Coast · Vík~2.5 hr

Waterfalls & black sand

Walk behind Seljalandsfoss, stand under Skógafoss, see the Sólheimajökull glacier tongue, then Dyrhólaey and the basalt columns of Reynisfjara black-sand beach.

Stay: Black Beach Suites (kitchenette) · Hotel Kría / Hotel Vík í Mýrdal
4Glaciers · Höfn~3 hr

Ice on every side

A guided glacier hike or Svartifoss at Skaftafell, the Fjaðrárgljúfur canyon, then the showstopper: Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon and the ice-strewn Diamond Beach. Vestrahorn at golden hour.

Stay: Fosshotel Glacier Lagoon · Arnanes Country Hotel
5Eastfjords~4 hr

The road less driven

A quiet, winding fjord coast most visitors skip — fishing villages, Petra's stone collection, reindeer in the hills — ending at Egilsstaðir or the rainbow street of Seyðisfjörður.

Stay: Hótel Eyvindará (log cabins) · Hótel 1001 Nótt
6Lake Mývatn~2.5 hr + explore

Volcano country

Detour to Dettifoss, Europe's most powerful waterfall, then Krafla and the Víti crater, the steaming Hverir mud pots, Dimmuborgir lava — and unwind in the Mývatn Nature Baths.

Stay: Vogafjós Farm Resort (working dairy farm) · Hótel Laxá
7Húsavík · Akureyri~2 hr

Whales & the capital of the north

Goðafoss on the way, then whale-watching from Húsavík (sightings near-guaranteed in July). Settle into walkable Akureyri — botanical garden, the landmark church, the Forest Lagoon.

Stay: Hótel Kea · Berjaya Akureyri
8West across the north~4.5 hr

The long, beautiful transfer

The biggest driving day — sweeping empty landscapes, the seal-shaped Hvítserkur sea stack, turf-house history. Land near Borgarnes or push onto Snæfellsnes for an early start tomorrow.

Stay: The Bookstore Family Apartment · Hótel Búðir (iconic)
9SnæfellsnesLoop day

"Iceland in miniature"

Everything the country does on one peninsula: photogenic Kirkjufell and its waterfall, Snæfellsjökull glacier-volcano, the Arnarstapi–Hellnar coastal walk, Djúpalónssandur beach, the black church at Búðir.

Stay: repeat last night's base, or edge toward Reykjavík
10DepartKEF ~2 hr

Last soak, then home

Drive back toward Keflavík (via scenic Hvalfjörður or a final Reykjavík morning). Time it for one last thermal soak or a bakery stop before your flight.

Stay: —

Advisor notes & logistics

  • Book early: peak-summer rooms go 6–9 months out; the Höfn/glacier-lagoon area sells first. Lock lodging by fall 2026.

The day-by-day

Based in Canmore/Banff so you unpack once. The Icefields Parkway runs as a day trip; Jasper is optional (still rebuilding from the 2024 wildfire).

1Arrive · CanmoreYYC 1.5 hr

Fly in, drive to the mountains

Land at Calgary, pick up the SUV, drive to Canmore (peaks within the hour). Settle in, stock the condo kitchen, stroll Canmore's Main Street for a first dinner.

Stay: Basecamp Suites Canmore · The Malcolm · Fairmont Banff Springs
2Banff townsitelocal

Get your bearings

Banff Gondola up Sulphur Mountain, Bow Falls and Surprise Corner, Lake Minnewanka (cruise or Stewart Canyon walk), and the Johnston Canyon falls hike. Banff Upper Hot Springs to close.

Stay: same base
3Lake Louise & Moraine~1 hr each way

The two famous lakes

Reserved early shuttle in (you can't drive to Moraine anymore). Canoe on Lake Louise, hike to the Lake Agnes Tea House or Plain of Six Glaciers, then the Rockpile over Moraine and the Valley of the Ten Peaks.

Stay: Baker Creek by Basecamp (cabins) · Post Hotel / Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise
4Yoho NP (BC)~1 hr

Over the divide, fewer crowds

Into British Columbia for Emerald Lake (canoe or the easy loop), towering Takakkaw Falls, the Natural Bridge, and the Spiral Tunnels — a quieter counterpoint to Lake Louise.

Stay: Lake Louise base, or back to Canmore
5Icefields Parkway~2.5 hr each way

One of the world's great drives

Bow Lake, the wolf-head overlook at Peyto Lake, then the Columbia Icefield — ride the Ice Explorer onto the Athabasca Glacier and walk the glass-floored Skywalk. Turn back, or push to Jasper if adding it.

Stay: Canmore/Lake Louise (or Jasper if extending)
6Big hike daylocal

Get up into the alpine

The Sunshine gondola to the wildflower meadows, or Larch Valley & Sentinel Pass (stunning in late season) or Healy Pass. The day you and Carson will remember — pick the effort level for everyone.

Stay: Canmore/Banff base
7Kananaskis~30–60 min

The locals' backyard

South into Kananaskis and Spray Lakes: Grassi Lakes, the Ha Ling Peak climb, big reservoir views, and the best wildlife odds of the trip. Or swap in white-water rafting on the Kicking Horse.

Stay: Canmore base
8Choose your adventureflex

A flex day

Options: a via ferrata at Mt Norquay, horseback riding, canoeing the Bow River and Vermilion Lakes, or a full Jasper overnight loop. Or return to a favorite lake at sunrise before the crowds.

Stay: Canmore/Banff base
9Slow morning + buffertoward YYC

Last mountain morning

Sunrise at Vermilion Lakes, the Cave & Basin, Banff Avenue shopping, the Bow River trail. Drive toward Calgary in the evening and overnight near the airport.

Stay: Calgary airport hotel (or one more Canmore night)
10DepartYYC

Fly home

Depart Calgary. Build in ~2.5 hours — U.S. customs preclearance happens in Calgary, so you clear before you fly.

The day-by-day

Sequenced for altitude: you sleep in the lower Sacred Valley first and only tackle Cusco (11,150 ft) and the high day-hikes once you're acclimatized.

1LimaAirport 40 min

Arrive in the capital

Land in Lima (usually evening) and settle into clifftop Miraflores. A relaxed first night — a ceviche dinner if you're up for it.

Stay: Casa Andina (Miraflores) · JW Marriott Lima · Belmond Miraflores Park
2Lima → Sacred ValleyFly ~1.5 hr

Straight down to acclimatize

A morning in Lima (Miraflores boardwalk, arty Barranco, the Larco Museum), then fly to Cusco and transfer down into the Sacred Valley — lower and gentler on the lungs than the city.

Stay: Tierra Viva Urubamba · Tambo del Inka / Sol y Luna · Belmond Rio Sagrado
3Sacred Valleyvalley

Markets, salt pans, terraces

The Pisac market and hilltop ruins, the cascading Maras salt pans, and the concentric agricultural terraces of Moray. An easy pace that doubles as altitude prep.

Stay: same base
4Ollantaytambovalley

A living Inca town

Climb the great Ollantaytambo fortress, wander streets laid out five centuries ago, visit a weaving cooperative, and keep it relaxed at the hotel before tomorrow's train.

Stay: same base, or El Albergue (Ollantaytambo station)
5→ Machu PicchuTrain ~1.5 hr

Into the cloud forest

The scenic train down the Urubamba gorge to Aguas Calientes. Settle in, and soak tired legs in the town's hot springs before the big day.

Stay: Casa Andina MP · SUMAQ / Inkaterra Machu Picchu Pueblo · Belmond Sanctuary Lodge
6Machu Picchu → CuscoTrain + drive

The wonder itself

An early bus up for a guided tour of the citadel (timed entry; Circuit 2 for the classic experience), with the option to climb Huayna Picchu. Afternoon train back and transfer to Cusco.

Stay: Tierra Viva Cusco · JW Marriott El Convento / Palacio del Inka · Palacio Nazarenas
7Cuscocity

The Inca capital

Now acclimatized: Qorikancha (Temple of the Sun), the Cathedral and Plaza de Armas, the artsy San Blas quarter, bustling San Pedro market, and the massive Sacsayhuamán walls above town.

Stay: same base
8Choose your adventureflex

Go high, or go to the jungle

A day hike to turquoise Humantay Lake or the striped Rainbow Mountain (both very high — only now that you're acclimatized), or fly to Puerto Maldonado to begin a 2-night Amazon-lodge extension.

Stay: Cusco base (or an Amazon lodge)
9Cusco / Amazonflex

A final full day

More Cusco — Chinchero, a cooking class, the food scene — or a full Amazon day of canopy walks, an oxbow-lake paddle, and wildlife spotting.

Stay: Cusco base (or Amazon lodge)
10DepartFly via Lima

Fly home

Fly Cusco → Lima and connect home (or route out of Puerto Maldonado via Lima if you did the Amazon).

Stay: —

Advisor notes & logistics

  • Altitude is the real planning item: Cusco sits at ~11,150 ft. Sleeping in the Sacred Valley first (lower) is the whole reason the days are ordered this way — hydrate, take it easy early, and save Rainbow Mountain/Humantay for after you've adjusted.
  • Permits sell out: Machu Picchu is timed-entry and guide-required, and books up 4–6 months ahead; the Inca Trail (if you ever want it) goes even earlier and is capped daily. Lock dates early.
  • Bonvoy payoff: the premium tier can run largely on Marriott properties, so your Lifetime Platinum status and points genuinely reduce cost here.
  • Fit: the richest of the three for culture and history, and a true "we did that" trip — adventure and heritage together.
  • Honest catch: more moving parts than a road trip (flights, trains, transfers) and altitude to respect. Worth it, but it's the least "unpack once and relax" of the three.
