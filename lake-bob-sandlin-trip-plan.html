☀️ Saturday — Full Day at Camp
Breakfast
Cinnamon Apple Porridge
Warm apple crisp in a camp bowl · One pot per 2 people

Rich oats simmered with canned apple pie filling until thick and fragrant. Topped by each person to their liking — granola for crunch, honey and coconut milk for richness. Smells like a bakery in the middle of the woods.

🔥 How to Cook
1
Bring water to boil across all 3 stoves. Use one pot per 2 people. Add ½ cup dry quick oats per person and stir.
2
Stir in a generous spoonful of canned apple pie filling per serving plus a pinch of cinnamon. Simmer 2 minutes until thick and glossy.
3
Spoon into bowls. Set out the toppings bar — everyone builds their own bowl.
🌶 Spice Dial — Everything is mild as cooked. Toppings bar: granola, hot honey, coconut milk drizzle, cinnamon, dried mango strips. All optional, all on the side.
Trail Lunch
Italian Wilderness Charcuterie Flatbreads
No cooking · Packed in zip bags · Eaten on the hike

A proper Italian charcuterie spread assembled on a rock mid-trail. Each person packs their own zip bag before heading out. Looks like something from a wine bar. Takes 3 minutes to pack.

📦 How to Pack & Assemble
1
Before leaving camp, divide into 6 individual zip bags: a handful of Triscuits, several salami slices, and shards of aged gouda.
2
On the trail: Triscuit → salami → gouda shard. Eat immediately.
3
Optional: drizzle of hot honey over everything. Changes the whole thing.
🌶 Spice Dial — Base is completely mild. Hot honey is the only heat element and is fully optional. Pack it separately so each person decides.
★ Showcase Dinner
Thai Coconut Peanut Curry Noodle Bowls
With crispy caramelized chicken · All 3 stoves running · The main event

A rich, fragrant coconut peanut broth with crispy caramelized chicken over ramen noodles. The base is completely mild — the spice station lets each person finish their own bowl however they want. This is the meal everyone will be talking about on the drive home.

🔥 3-Stove Cook — Run Simultaneously
🔥 Stove 1 — Broth

2 cans coconut milk + half a bottle of Thai peanut sauce + juice of 2 limes. Whisk over medium heat until smooth and simmering. No chili in the base.

🔥 Stove 2 — Noodles

Boil ramen noodle bricks in water (discard seasoning packets). Drain. Toss with a few drops of Thai peanut sauce to prevent sticking. Hold warm.

🔥 Stove 3 — Chicken

Drain canned chicken. Press into a dry hot pan — don't touch for 3–4 min to form a crust. Flip. Finish with a drizzle of hot honey to caramelize. No chili in the cook.

🥣 Build the Bowl

Noodles → ladle of coconut peanut broth → crispy chicken crumbled over top → crushed peanuts → green onions → squeeze of lime. Then hit the spice station.

🌶 Spice Station — Each Person Finishes Their Own Bowl
Sriracha · Extra lime wedges · Extra Thai peanut sauce
Mild bowl = rich creamy peanut comfort. Full heat bowl = complex Thai curry energy. Same pot of broth for everyone.
Dessert
Coconut Milk Rice Pudding
Creamy, tropical, one pot · Stove only · No fire needed

Rich and deeply comforting — instant rice simmered in coconut milk until thick and pudding-like, finished with cinnamon and a drizzle of hot honey. Topped with dried mango for a tropical lift. Uses the same coconut milk already in your kit.

🔥 How to Cook
1
Combine 1 can coconut milk + ½ can water in a pot over medium heat. Stir in ¾ cup instant rice and a pinch of cinnamon. Bring to a gentle simmer.
2
Stir frequently for 8–10 minutes until the rice absorbs the coconut milk and the mixture thickens to a creamy pudding consistency. Add a splash more water if it gets too thick.
3
Spoon into bowls. Top with dried mango strips and a drizzle of hot honey. Eat warm.
🌅 Sunday — Pack Up & Head Home
Quick Breakfast
Everything Bagel Salami Stacks
No stoves · No cleanup · Boots already on

Zero cooking. Camp is being broken down while everyone eats. Everything packs out in the bags it came in. Done in 10 minutes.

1
Mini bagel or Triscuit → shard of aged gouda → fold of hard salami.
2
Shake everything bagel seasoning over the top. Add a slice of cucumber.
3
Optional hot honey drizzle. Eat standing. Hike out.
🌶 Spice Dial — Hot honey is the only heat. Base is 100% mild. Fully optional.
