The shape of the trip

Depart DFW late on Day 0 → land Rome Day 1. Three hotel nights in the city, board Viking the afternoon of Day 4, and disembark in Athens on Day 11. Fly home the same day, or tack on a night or two for the Acropolis.

Cruise  Viking Journey to Antiquities — 8 days / 7 nights, Rome (Civitavecchia) to Athens (Piraeus), calling at Naples, Messina, Crete and Ephesus with two restful days at sea.

What Viking includes

  • A private veranda in every stateroom, on a small ~930-guest ship — no kids, no casino
  • All meals, plus beer, wine & soft drinks with lunch and dinner
  • Wi-Fi, and one guided shore excursion in every port
  • Enrichment lectures from a Resident Historian to set up each ancient site

Not included  Airfare, gratuities (~$18/person/day), your Rome days, and any optional excursions beyond the one bundled per port.

Where it moves

Because Viking bundles meals, drinks with meals, Wi-Fi and a tour in every port, your onboard add-ons stay small. The two levers that actually move the total:

  • Airfare — peak-summer, open-jaw seats out of DFW; booking Viking Air or watching AA fares matters most.
  • Optional tours — the included tour covers each port; upgrades (private Pompeii, Etna, Terrace Houses) are where extra spend lands.

Good to know

Katelyn's and Carson's passports expire 31 May 2028 — before this trip. Renew both (now as adult 10-year passports), with at least 3 months' validity past your return date, well ahead of summer 2029. Tony's (Jul 2032) and Kristin's (Dec 2032) are fine.

Booking & how to hold it

Viking's 2029 season is open now. A $25-per-person deposit holds your two cabins — about $100 total — with final payment not due until 13 January 2028.

Watch  Confirm whether the sale fare is refundable — some Viking promo fares aren't. Given your 2029 school calendar isn't published yet, a refundable hold is worth it. Viking doesn't drop your fare later, so booking early locks the lowest price.

Ages & timing

Viking is 18+. Carson turns 18 on 23 June 2029, so the sailing must depart on or after that date — target a July departure. Katelyn will be 21. Once Carson's 18 he can add Global Entry; yours renew in Feb 2030.

Entry & on the ground

EU entry  By 2029, expect to need ETIAS, the EU's online travel authorization (quick, small fee) — check the current requirement a few months out.

Heat  Mediterranean summer is hot — Pompeii and Ephesus can top 100°F. Do the ruins early, and carry water, hats and real shoes.
